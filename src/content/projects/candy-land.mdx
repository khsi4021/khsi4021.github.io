<TabPanel id="overview">

Each tile is a struct with a colour and a type. `Board` renders the track with ANSI colour codes, `Player` tracks gold, stamina and candies, and `games` holds the mini-games and riddles loaded from `riddles.txt`. The goal is to reach the castle first while holding the most gold, stamina and candies.

It was my first project split across several classes and files, and my first practice reading game content from data files.
