This experiment investigated regenerative power generation: using Peltier coolers and the temperature gradients found in space to produce electrical power. I designed and developed a suborbital payload to test the idea, and the payload reached the Kármán line.

The work had three parts.

1. **Payload.** I designed and developed the suborbital payload around the Peltier coolers.
2. **Ground test.** Before launch I ran vacuum-chamber tests with liquid-nitrogen cooling, to reproduce space thermal conditions and characterise the payload's power generation.
3. **Flight comparison.** After the flight I correlated the vacuum-chamber results with the post-flight telemetry and overlaid the two, to check the initial predictions and to assess how the system performed under actual flight conditions.
