Project REGENESIS was the University of Colorado Boulder entry in NASA's 2025–2026 RASC-AL competition. The team developed a concept for lunar in-situ resource utilisation (ISRU) infrastructure, working with aerospace faculty advisors.

I was the team lead from August 2025 to April 2026. The team had 16 members from several engineering disciplines.

My own technical work was simulation. I ran ANSYS simulations to assess how durable the launchpad would be under two kinds of loading: the plume of a lunar lander, and the landing loads.
