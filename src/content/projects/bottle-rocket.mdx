<TabPanel id="overview">

The state vector is [height, downrange position, v<sub>z</sub>, v<sub>x</sub>, rocket mass, air volume, air mass]. The thrust phase switches on the state:

- **Water expulsion.** The air expands isentropically and pushes water through the throat (discharge coefficient 0.78).
- **Air expansion.** The remaining compressed air exhausts, choked or not depending on the pressure ratio.
- **Ballistic coast.** Drag only (C<sub>D</sub> = 0.425).

The rocket leaves a 0.5 m launch rail at 40°.

<TabPanel id="results">

Across 40–70 psi, the range grows almost linearly with launch gauge pressure, by roughly 10 m per 10 psi, and the apogee rises from about 16 m to about 28 m (read from the figure). In the air-expansion phase the model checks whether the throat flow is choked, using the critical pressure ratio, and switches its exit conditions accordingly.

</TabPanel>
