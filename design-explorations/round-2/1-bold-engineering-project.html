A missing term

Marched downstream, the heat flux fell only 6 % over 45°, against about 20 % in the paper's DNS. At Pr = 1, total enthalpy must satisfy (CS′)′ + fS′ = 0 for any β. The printed energy equation satisfies that only when β·Me = 0, because it omits the pressure-gradient work term.

(C g′)′ + Pr f g′ = −Pr C(γ−1)Me² f″² − Pr β(γ−1)Me² f′(f′² − g)restored

With the term restored, the identity holds to 10⁻⁶, and the 30° heat flux moves from 4.15 to 3.55 W/cm², against ≈ 3.6 in the DNS.
