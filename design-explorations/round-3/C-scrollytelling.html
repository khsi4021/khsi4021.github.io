Chapter 1 · Laminar heating and PANT-RT transition onset on a Mach-6 cylinder.

i. The problem

Where does roughness trip a hypersonic boundary layer?

Turbulence multiplies wall heating. Predicting where it starts on a blunt body means computing laminar heating first, then applying a published transition criterion.

ii. The method

A compressible similarity boundary layer, marched downstream

Illingworth variables, a five-state BVP solved with SciPy, local-similarity marching, and the PANT family of correlations. 144 tests pin the physics.

iii. The check

−1.45 %

against Fay–Riddell, plus a missing term found

The printed energy equation omits a pressure-work term once β·Me ≠ 0. I restored it and verified the fix with an exact identity.

Chapter 2 · Each burn moves the miss point outside the 3.2 × 10⁻⁶ contour.

i. The problem

Most conjunction warnings are routine. Which ones aren't?

Analysts judge data quality, interpret collision probability and design maneuvers. The aim: automate the routine cases and flag the ambiguous ones.

ii. The method

Parse, score, forecast, maneuver

CDM parsing and credibility scoring, an XGBoost risk forecast, and MATLAB minimum-ΔV burns verified by propagation and Monte Carlo.

iii. The check

567 / 567

maneuvers meet the NASA target

The collision-probability code matches NASA CARA to 8.9 × 10⁻⁸. The ML model's tie with its baseline is reported as-is.

Chapter 3 · Dutch-roll response: nonlinear simulator and analytic model overlap.

i. The problem

Last year's simulator was 65 % off on the Dutch roll

Our capstone UAV needed a simulator the whole team could trust.

ii. The method

A Simulink 6-DOF model generated from a script

Linearise the full nonlinear model and compare its modes with an analytic model built from the same derivatives.

iii. The check

0.09 %

largest modal error

All five Boeing 747 modes, plus doublet responses within 0.5–0.9 % NRMSE.

Chapter 4 · Which wings close at the design mission.

i. The problem

Two weight models, a factor of 1.7 apart

At a fixed 55 lb MTOW, the battery is whatever weight is left, so the empty-weight estimate decides whether the mission closes.

ii. The method

A survey of real electric UAVs, held-out benchmark

Grade the sources, fit a regression, then test it blind on an aircraft kept out of the fit.

iii. The check

+1.7 %

blind error, against +29 % for the textbook model

That model feeds the flap model, design sweeps and mission envelope.
