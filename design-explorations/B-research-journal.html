Heating, transition, and checking the answer.

Notes from an aerospace engineering student working toward CFD and hypersonics research.

Abstract. A laminar stagnation-heating and roughness-transition estimator for a Mach-6 cylinder. It solves the compressible similarity boundary layer in Illingworth variables, marches downstream under local similarity, and applies the PANT family of correlations. It agrees with Fay–Riddell to −1.45 %. The benchmark gap against the source paper's Table 3 is reported and diagnosed rather than tuned away.

The energy equation as printed omits a pressure-work term that matters once the edge Mach number is finite. With it restored, the Pr = 1 total-enthalpy identity holds to 10⁻⁶:

(C g′)′ + Pr f g′ = −Pr C (γ−1) Me² f″² − Pr β (γ−1) Me² f′(f′² − g) (1)
